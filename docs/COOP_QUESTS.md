# Co-op quests: foundations, Power Problems, five optional quests

This page covers the quest framework added in build 0xC0170131, the quest built on it then (New Mauville, "Power Problems"), the five optional quests added in build 0xC0170132, and the audit and test matrix of build 0xC0170138 (section 4). Player-facing text is in [COOP.md](COOP.md#quests).

## 1. Foundations

| Piece | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quest journal | Start menu, **CO-OP**, **JOURNAL** (linked or not) | Each save's quest state, one step per page: what is done, what is next, and whether the partner did part of it. Built by `CoopQuest_BufferJournal` (`src/coop_quest.c`). |
| Habitat and evolution hints | The field researcher in the Petalburg, Mauville, Fortree and Lilycove Pokémon Centers | Where each added Pokémon lives and how unusual evolutions work. Generated by `tools/coop_field_guide.py`, which now also lists JOLTIK and PORYGON and the APPLIANCE ROOM ROTOM. |
| Badge-gated evolution shop | The **POWER SHOP** clerk in Mauville City, by the Gym | Repeatable. Opens when New Mauville's generator is off. Its stock grows at the sixth, seventh and eighth badges (table below). |
| Optional duo trainers | VOLT and FUSE in New Mauville | Linked, on the same map, with the Twin Beacon on, VOLT offers a 2-on-2 against himself and FUSE. NO, or any other situation, is a solo fight against VOLT alone. FUSE can also be fought alone. |
| Per-player rewards | `CoopSave.rewardsClaimed` (bits `COOP_REWARD_*` in `include/constants/coop.h`) plus the stock flags | Each save claims each reward once, whoever did the work. A bit is never cleared or copied across the link. |
| Live quest sync | `DMSG_QUEST` deal blocks (`src/coop_deal.c`) | Small quest values sent in order and resent until acknowledged, while linked. Used for the breakers. |
| Shared milestones | The story ledger in `src/coop.c` | Facts both saves should agree on, added only, written at a map change. `COOP_STORY_POWER_RESTORED` is the second one. |

### Shared and personal state

- **Personal (each save keeps its own):**
  - quest progress while not linked;
  - trainer flags;
  - static encounters;
  - item balls;
  - journal pages;
  - rewards;
  - parties, bags and money.

  Nothing personal is ever copied from the partner.
- **Shared while linked (live):** quest values sent with `DMSG_QUEST`.
  - A live change replaces the partner's value, so a trip is a trip for both.
  - On a new link each side reports its value and both keep the higher one, so progress made apart is not lost.
- **Shared milestones (story ledger):** world facts such as "the generator is off".
  - They only add, never remove.
  - They are applied to the other save at its next map change, so nobody is moved or interrupted.
  - A save that receives a milestone it did not earn gets the matching catch-up state, and keeps its own rewards to claim.

## 2. New Mauville: Power Problems

### Walkthrough

1. **WATTSON** (Mauville City, after the fifth badge) gives the BASEMENT KEY as before. He also explains that every surge trips three circuit breakers. The MAIN SWITCH won't work until all three are reset, and a MAINTENANCE LOG by the stairs gives the order.
2. **The MAINTENANCE LOG**, on the wall north of the entrance stairs, gives the order: STORAGE, then SERVICE, then CONTROL.
3. **The STORAGE breaker** is on the wall of the crate room (west). A ROLYCOLY (Lv 26) is rolled up against it. Battle it, catch it, or run; either way it leaves the panel.
4. **The SERVICE breaker** is in the big workshop. A JOLTIK (Lv 26) is chewing its wiring.
5. **The CONTROL breaker** is in the generator room, top right.
   - Each panel turns from orange (TRIPPED) to green (reset).
   - A panel pressed out of order trips every breaker again.
6. **VOLT** stands on the MAIN SWITCH (Guitarist: Magneton 40, Galvantula 40, Luxray 42 with a Sitrus Berry). He can be fought before or after the breakers. Beaten, he steps off the switch.
   - With a partner on the map, linked, and the Beacon on, he calls **FUSE** (Pokémaniac: Electrode 40, Manectric 41). Both players can fight the pair as a co-op multi battle.
   - FUSE can also be fought alone.
7. **The MAIN SWITCH** stops the generator once all three breakers are reset. Before that it shows how many are done. Stopping it does three things:
   - VOLT and FUSE leave.
   - The jammed shutter of the **APPLIANCE ROOM** (east wing) opens.
   - The **POWER SHOP** opens in Mauville.
8. **The APPLIANCE ROOM** holds this save's own ROTOM (Lv 32). It stays until it is caught, and its appliances explain the ROTOM CATALOG.
9. **WATTSON** gives TM24 Thunderbolt once per save, then tells you about the POWER SHOP.

**Optional trail.** Three torn pages tell of a sealed control chamber:
- page 1: the console in the top-left corridor;
- page 2: between the crates in the south-west corridor;
- page 3: the desk drawer in the APPLIANCE ROOM.

With all three read and the generator off, the panel in the generator room's south wall opens. Inside are this save's PORYGON (Lv 30, stays until caught), an UP-GRADE, and a last research log. Before that the panel says it is screwed shut, or that it hums with current while the generator runs.

### Co-op behaviour

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Both linked, anywhere | A breaker reset or trip by either player changes the other save at once. A partner in New Mauville sees the consoles change and a note ("MISTY: breakers 2/3"). |
| Split tasks | One player can clear STORAGE while the other waits at SERVICE, and so on. The order is shared, not who presses. ROLYCOLY and JOLTIK are each save's own, so each player clears the panel in front of them on their own save. |
| VOLT with the partner here, Beacon on | The duo question. YES invites the partner; the partner's own YES starts a co-op multi battle. Both saves record both wins, and VOLT steps aside on both. |
| VOLT otherwise, or NO | VOLT alone, single battle. |
| One player throws the switch | That save: generator off. The other save gets "power restored" at its next map change (catch-up, `FLAG_PP_CATCHUP`): crates gone, VOLT gone, shop open, breakers green. WATTSON still pays that player, with a line saying the partner got there first. |
| Staggered arrival (one finished long before) | Same as the row above, at the first map change after the two link. |
| Reconnect mid-quest | Both report their breaker count; both keep the higher one. |
| Save and reload | Everything is in the save: the breakers (`VAR_PP_BREAKERS`), the generator (`VAR_NEW_MAUVILLE_STATE`), trainer flags, pages, panel, encounters, rewards. The consoles and panel are redrawn on load. |
| Duplicates | WATTSON pays once per save (stock flag plus `COOP_REWARD_PP_WATTSON`). The chamber is recorded once (`COOP_REWARD_PP_CHAMBER`). Item balls and encounters are per save. A milestone never removes anything. |

## 3. Five optional quests (build 0xC0170132)

All five follow the same rules:

- **Solo first.** Every step can be done by one player, linked or not. No quest waits for, or needs, a second player.
- **Shared outcome, personal rewards.** Clues, crates and inscriptions found by either player count for both (`DMSG_QUEST`, OR-merged live, and merged again on every new link). A finished quest is a ledger milestone; the other save gets it at its next map change, with a catch-up flag and a short line instead of the full scene. Each save claims its own reward once (`COOP_REWARD_*`); a full bag defers the reward and the giver keeps it.
- **No softlocks.** Partner objects never block a critical tile (they are passable); duo fights fall back to solo if the partner can't or won't join; nothing is gated on linking, invitations or reconnection.
- **Journal.** CO-OP, JOURNAL lists POWER PROBLEMS, MISSING RESEARCHER, DESERT CARAVAN, MIRA AND SOREN, COASTAL CLEANUP and BEACON HISTORY, each with the next step and a hint.
- **Shops and hints.** Each finished quest opens a stall at Mauville's POWER SHOP (after its own stock): ICE GOODS (Shoal), SEA GOODS (coast), DESERT GOODS (caravan), BEACON GOODS (shrine). The field researchers' habitat guide lists the quest Pokémon (Alolan Vulpix, Alolan Grimer, Skrelp, Mudbray, Scraggy, Sigilyph).

### Behaviour notes found in testing

- A co-op story battle (`Coop_StartStoryBattle`) resumes the **host's** script after a win; the **guest** returns to the field with no script running. Guest-side outcomes are therefore applied by flags (the battle's flag on both saves, then map `ON_RESUME`/`ON_TRANSITION` handlers or the next talk), never by script lines after the battle.
- If both players talk to the same duo NPC at once, one becomes the host and the other's open message closes as they join; nothing is lost.

## 4. Audit and test matrix (build 0xC0170138)

Every stage requested for the six quests was already in the scripts (sections 2 and 3); the audit found none missing. One addition: the Shoal Cave journal page now says the paths down are uncovered at low tide (3:00–8:59 and 15:00–20:59). No quest defect was found by the tests below, so no quest script was changed otherwise.

### Shared and personal state (unchanged, restated)

| Shared (both saves, live while linked, merged on relink) | Personal (each save) |
| --- | --- |
| Breakers (`VAR_PP_BREAKERS`), clues, crates, inscriptions, shrine gates, twin meetings heard (`DMSG_QUEST`) | Trainer flags (except duo fights, which both record), static encounters (Rotom, Porygon, Alolan Vulpix, Alolan Grimer, Sigilyph), item balls, rewards (`COOP_REWARD_*`), journal |
| Milestones: generator off, Isla rescued, caravan resupplied, beach clean, shrine open/solved (added only; applied at the other save's next map change, with a catch-up flag and its own reward) | Bags, parties, money |
