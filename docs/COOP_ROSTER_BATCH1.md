# Co-op roster, batch 1 (114 species)

Generated by `tools/coop_roster/manifest.py` from the ROM sources; `tools/coop_roster/audit.py` checks the same data.

Before this batch none of the 114 species was obtainable in this ROM (audit of the pre-batch source: 0/114). After it: 114/114 obtainable, 45 with a direct source (wild slot or partner gift), 69 by evolution. 0 were already obtainable; 0 had access repaired.

Species IDs are unchanged; new placements reuse existing encounter slots (duplicates of a species already in the table), so table sizes and existing species stay as they were.

## Partner Pokémon (starter families)

A guest Trainer at the left of the Pokémon Centre 1F counter offers one of three starters per day (a daily flag, cleared with the other daily flags). Each eligible player takes their own; a full party sends it to the PC. The guest says what is needed when the gate is not met.

| Kanto | Rustboro | 1 | 10 | Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle |
| Sinnoh | Lavaridge | 4 | 18 | Turtwig, Chimchar, Piplup |
| Unova | Fortree | 6 | 25 | Snivy, Tepig, Oshawott |
| Kalos | Mossdeep | 7 | 30 | Chespin, Fennekin, Froakie |
