## 1. Roster batch 2

- **Newly obtainable: 123 species.** All 114 of the batch (0 were obtainable before; 52 with a direct source, 62 by evolution) and 9 regional entries that stock Emerald only offered by trade (Surskit, Masquerain, Meditite, Medicham, Budew, Roselia, Roserade, Obstagoon, Cursola). Zangoose and Lunatone, previously only met as co-op wild pairs, now have solo slots; the two Hoenn starters a save did not pick now come from a Fallarbor guest.
- **Manifest:** [COOP_ROSTER_BATCH2.md](COOP_ROSTER_BATCH2.md) (readable) and `docs/coop_roster_manifest.json` (all 680 obtainable species: method, place, unlock, levels, rate, evolution resources and where they are sold, renewable, verification), generated by `tools/coop_roster_audit/roster.py` and `batch_doc.py`.
- **Partner Pokémon:** Alola (Slateport, 2 badges, Lv 14), Galar (Petalburg, 5, Lv 22), Paldea (Sootopolis, 8, Lv 35), Hoenn (Fallarbor, 3, Lv 16). One a day per save.
- **Fossils:** the Route 114 Fossil Maniac gives any of the eleven fossils once a day; DEVON revives all of them (Lv 20). No permanent either/or.
- **Habitats:** 39 duplicate-slot placements for the batch and 8 for the repairs; no table lost a species, no earlier placement or revamp was changed.
- **Evolutions:** all solo. POWER SHOP adds the seven Sweets and the Soothe Bell, and the Cracked/Chipped Pot from six badges. Engine fixes: short clockwise spins never triggered a spin evolution (`SPIN_CW_SHORT` equals `EVO_NONE`), and Cramorant's Gulping form showed only on the host's screen in co-op battles.
- **Day Care eggs** are now counted by the audit (Poliwag, Snover, Roggenrola and others hatch from their evolved forms; one egg hatched in play).
- **Forms:** cosmetic forms are not separate species. Supported: Toxtricity (Amped, Low Key), Sinistea/Polteageist (Phony, Antique), 12 Alcremie forms, the Alolan and Galarian forms placed so far, Shellos/Gastrodon, Pumpkaboo/Gourgeist, Lycanroc. Deerling/Sawsbuck are Spring only. Battle forms Eiscue, Morpeko, Cramorant (Gulping) were played on both co-op screens.
- Not added: legendary encounters, Mega Milotic, a balance overhaul.
