# Encounter variety: visitors by time of day and weather

Section 2 of the collection update, added after build 0xC0170141. Waiting at a Pokémon Center for another time of day (section 8) is in [COOP_TIME.md](COOP_TIME.md).

## What the player meets

Some Pokémon visit a map only at one time of day (MORNING, DAY, EVENING, NIGHT) or in one weather (rain, falling ash). While a visitor's condition holds, **1 in 4** encounters in its area (grass or cave, surfing, a rod, Rock Smash) is a visitor instead of a Pokémon from the map's own table.

- **Level:** the table slot's own level, so levels, Repel and Keen Eye work as before.
- **Nothing is removed:** every Pokémon found on a map before is still found there, at every time.
- **The time of day** is what the game shows: the clock, plus any rest at a Pokémon Center. While linked, it is the link master's shared time.
- **The weather** is the map's weather now. For example, Route 119 and Route 123 rain on some days, and Route 113 has falling ash in its middle.
- **Co-op:** a co-op wild battle is generated by the game that met it and sent to the partner, so both screens show the same visitor.
- **Not in** the Battle Pike, the Battle Pyramid, or a quest's own fishing spot.

**Why visitors, not the engine's time-of-day tables.** The expansion's own time-of-day tables (`OW_TIME_OF_DAY_ENCOUNTERS`) stay off. Turning them on changes the Pokédex AREA page for every species (per-time pages, without the co-op acquisition hints) and every reader of the tables. The visitor layer adds variety without touching any existing table or page.

## WILDLIFE REPORT and hints

- **The report.** In a Pokémon Center, CO-OP > WAIT HERE > **REPORT** pages through the visitors of the maps near that town, one page per map and condition: "ROUTE 101, NIGHT: / Hoothoot, ???". A visitor this save has seen is named; one not seen yet is ???, or, when it is alone on its page, a rumor ("rumor of a ladybug in the sun"). Then: "Rest here to meet them at another time. Weather changes by itself." Fourteen towns have a report; elsewhere, "There's no news of visiting POKéMON around here."
- **Pokédex AREA.** The page marks a visitor's maps too, whatever the time.
- **Acquisition manifest.** `docs/coop_roster_manifest.json` lists each visitor as a wild source ("grass/cave, visitor at night"). After this change it still reports 908 of 908 species obtainable and 0 issues. Visitors make seven species available earlier than before:

## The visitors

The source is `src/data/coop_visitors.json`; `tools/coop_encounters/visitors_to_header.py` makes `src/data/coop_visitors.h` (run by make). The generator refuses:

- an unknown map, species, area, condition or rod;
- a map without that kind of table;
- a visitor already in that table;
- a missing hint;
- a report with no visitors;
- a report page wider than the message box.

## Tests (harness)

The 100% chance in these tests is a fixture (`gCoopVisitorTestChance`); the game uses 25%.

| Scenario | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| `visitors_time` | At night on Route 101, a night visitor (Spinarak). The WILDLIFE REPORT in Oldale. After resting until MORNING: Ledyba, the only morning visitor. By DAY: no visitor |
| `visitors_weather_fish` | j52 saves. SUPER ROD on Route 122 at night: Frillish. Route 113 grass in falling ash (FIXTURE: the saved weather): a visitor (Vulpix). In sunshine: none |

**Not tested in play:**
- rain visitors (same code path as ash);
- surfing visitors;
- Rock Smash (none are defined);
- the Pokédex AREA page with visitors;
- the real 25% rate (only the fixed 100%).
