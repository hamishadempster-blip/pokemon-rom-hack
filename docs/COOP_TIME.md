# Time of day: waiting at a Pokémon Center

Section 8 of the collection update, added after build 0xC0170141. Time-of-day encounters (section 2) are described in [COOP_ENCOUNTERS.md](COOP_ENCOUNTERS.md).

## What the player does

On any Pokémon Center's ground floor, START > CO-OP > **WAIT HERE** (just above BACK; not offered anywhere else). The game says what time of day it is now ("It's NIGHT. Rest here until…") and offers MORNING, DAY, EVENING, NIGHT and CANCEL, plus **REAL TIME** once the player has rested.

The screen fades, and "ASH rested until MORNING." Resting always moves forward, to:

| Choice | Time reached | Why there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MORNING | 08:00 | Morning runs 06:00–10:00. At 06:00 the outdoor tint still looks like night; by 08:00 it looks like morning |
| DAY | 10:00 | Day runs 10:00–19:00 |
| EVENING | 19:20 | Evening lasts only an hour (19:00–20:00). At 19:00 the tint still looks like day |
| NIGHT | 21:00 | Night runs 20:00–06:00. By 21:00 the tint is fully night |

These hours are the expansion's `OW_TIMES_OF_DAY` GEN_8 periods; a static assert in `src/coop_time.c` keeps the table in step with them. Choosing the period it already is says "It's already NIGHT." and changes nothing.

## What changes and what does not

- **What follows the rest:** everything that asks the game for the time of day. That covers the outdoor tint, night music, time-of-day wild Pokémon (once section 2 turns them on), evolutions that need day or night, and moves and forms that depend on the time.
- **What does not move:** the cartridge clock, the day count, berries, daily events, tides and every timer kept by the real clock. So resting "into tomorrow" repeats nothing that happens once a day.
- **How it is stored:** one number per save, the minutes added to the time of day (`CoopSaveV5.periodShift`, 0–1439). It was reserved and zero, so there is no layout change, and it is saved with the next save of any kind. After resting, the time of day keeps running from the new point at the clock's pace.
- **Going back:** REAL TIME sets it back to 0, and the time of day follows the clock again.

## Linked

While linked, both games already show the link master's time of day (`src/coop_deal.c`, shared clock; the master now sends its time including any rest). So a rest is made together:

- **The partner is in the same Center:** the partner's game asks "ASH wants to rest here until MORNING. Rest together?". It uses the partner-request system (a deal of kind `COOP_DEAL_WAIT`: request and answer only; nothing is exchanged, escrowed or saved). On YES, each game shifts its own time so that both reach the chosen period together. They stay together after UNLINK, even with clocks hours apart. NO, a busy partner, B ("call off"), a timeout or a dropped link change nothing on either game.
- **The partner is elsewhere:** "You're linked: rest together. Meet your partner in this CENTER."
- **If the link drops just after the YES** (not tested), the partner may have rested while the asker did not. Each can rest again alone; nothing else depends on it.
- **Co-op wild battles** are generated by one game and sent to the other, so the two never see different Pokémon for one encounter, whatever their own times.
