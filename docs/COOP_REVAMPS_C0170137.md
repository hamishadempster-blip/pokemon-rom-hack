## Balance notes

- The proposed stats were applied as given; no adjustments. Base stat totals: Butterfree 445, Beedrill 465, Arbok 485, Parasect 460, Hypno 510, Furret 465, Lanturn 470, Kricketune 440, Cherrim 500, Lumineon 500, Maractus 480, Spidops 450 — all below the pseudo-legendary and boss tiers.
- **Sleep:** Butterfree no longer learns Sleep Powder by level (Compound Eyes would make it a near-certain sleep every turn with Tailwind behind it); Stun Spore and Poison Powder stay. Parasect keeps Spore (vanilla) at Speed 30, and Hypno keeps Hypnosis (60% accuracy); neither got speed control.
- **Support spread:** Heal Pulse went to two (Hypno, Lanturn), Pollen Puff to two (Butterfree, Maractus), Tailwind stays on Butterfree (36) and moved earlier on Lumineon (59 → 38), Encore to one (Kricketune), U-turn to four physical/utility Pokémon (Beedrill, Furret, Kricketune, Spidops), Knock Off to four. Helping Hand and Follow Me were already on Cherrim and Furret.
- **Removed:** Swagger (Hypno), Attract (Lumineon), Take Down (Lanturn, Cherrim), Slam and Amnesia (Furret), Absorb (Kricketune), Headbutt (Spidops), Solar Beam (Cherrim, replaced by the physical Solar Blade).
- **Telepathy and Pollen Puff:** a Telepathy Pokémon blocks its ally's Pollen Puff heal (the official rule; seen in testing). Hypno's Telepathy is its second slot, Insomnia its first, so a Hypno partnered with Butterfree or Maractus can use Insomnia.
- **Cherrim:** both battle forms (Overcast, Sunshine) carry the same stats and Grass/Fairy typing; Flower Gift changes it to the Sunshine form in the sun (checked in solo and co-op battles).
- **Owned Pokémon:** party stats are recalculated on CONTINUE (`Coop_RefreshPartyStats`, src/overworld.c), so a revamped Pokémon from an older save gets the new numbers without changing its IVs, EVs, nature, moves or ability slot; boxed Pokémon are recalculated when withdrawn (vanilla). Ability changes follow the slot an individual already has: with its first ability, an owned Beedrill now has Poison Touch, Parasect or Paras Dry Skin, Furret or Sentret Technician, Kricketune Technician, Lumineon or Finneon Storm Drain, Spidops or Tarountula Stakeout; with its second ability, Hypno or Drowzee has Telepathy, Lanturn or Chinchou Water Absorb. Moves an owned Pokémon already knows are kept. Species IDs are unchanged.
